# Attune demo walkthrough (about two minutes)

This is a proposed script, not a record of a completed demo. Confirm the deployed frontend and Worker contain the same version, then rehearse in a clean session. Show the actual data-state label throughout; never describe demo or cached data as a fresh live response.

1. **Problem (0:00–0:15):** “Independent venues make atmosphere and menu decisions every shift. Attune uses neighborhood taste signals to suggest ideas an operator can review.”
2. **Venue and constraints (0:15–0:40):** Select a venue and show the resolved location. Open “Tune your shift plan,” enter its actual hours, atmosphere, and an existing menu category. Explain that these details come from the operator.
3. **Qloo signal (0:40–1:05):** Show a returned place-to-artist connection, its actual affinity and the data-state label. Avoid promising a specific entity or score before verifying the response.
4. **Shift plan (1:05–1:30):** Show an open and a closed daypart and how the inputs affect the plan. Choose a specific recommendation supported by a visible artist signal.
5. **Why this? (1:30–1:55):** Open that daypart’s evidence. Explain the signal, operator inputs, source limitations, and that an affinity does not measure demand or outcomes. Show when an action has no supporting entity.
6. **Close (1:55–2:10):** “Attune turns taste signals into suggestions an operator can inspect and adapt.”

Rehearse Qloo-unavailable and incompatible-backend states separately. Do not claim pilot results, integrations, sales lift, or operator validation without evidence. Follow the competition rules before changing submitted materials.
