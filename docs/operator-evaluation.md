# Operator evaluation plan

This is a research protocol, not a report of completed interviews or measured impact.

Recruit three to five independent hospitality operators and obtain their consent to record anonymized notes. Ask each to select a venue they know, review its evidence and playbook, and identify actions they would try, reject, or change. Ask about opening hours, budget, stock, music licensing, and customer needs. Record a 1–5 rating for relevance, feasibility, specificity, and trust; ask for a concrete reason for each rating. Do not collect customer identities.

Record the venue, date, whether data was live, cached, or demo, and the exact product version. Compare feedback across operators and prioritize repeated issues. Do not represent a small qualitative sample as evidence of revenue impact.

For an optional pilot, agree in advance on one action, baseline, measurement window, and relevant external factors. Record both positive and negative observations, and avoid causal claims without an appropriate comparison.

## Demo verification checklist

- [ ] Confirm the public site and API serve the same intended version and the repository is public.
- [ ] In a fresh browser, resolve a known venue and inspect the selected area.
- [ ] Confirm signal names and scores against the underlying responses, and inspect evidence and assumptions.
- [ ] Change venue and verify that stale playbook and signals do not persist.
- [ ] Exercise Qloo, agent, and network failures; identify cached and demo data honestly.
- [ ] Repeat the journey on a phone and desktop and capture results without exposing keys.
- [ ] Check the competition rules before modifying a submitted project or demo.
